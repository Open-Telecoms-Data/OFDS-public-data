# Backbone National de Guinée #

This map was digitized from the publicly available network map of the Backbone National de Guinée at https://sogeb.net.gn/la-societe-de-gestion-et-dexploitation-du-backbone-national-sogeb-a-fibre-optique-est-un-reseau-federateur-dans-larriere-du-pays-afin-de-desservir-sur-litineraire-princip/ [archive copy](https://web.archive.org/web/20240724230406/https://sogeb.net.gn/la-societe-de-gestion-et-dexploitation-du-backbone-national-sogeb-a-fibre-optique-est-un-reseau-federateur-dans-larriere-du-pays-afin-de-desservir-sur-litineraire-princip/). It is not a perfect transcription as some routes and locations are hard to make out and/or geo-locate.  Routes are approximations based on the assumption that the fibre follows road infrastructure.  In cases where the name of a Point of Presence was too blurred to make out, the name "PoP" was assigned.  It is not clear to me what the red nodes are.  They have been left out.

-- Steve Song, September 2026
